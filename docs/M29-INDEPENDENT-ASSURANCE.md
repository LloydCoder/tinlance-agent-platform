# M29 — Independent Enterprise Assurance

M29 is the final finite engineering-assurance gate.

An assurance report records an assessor, explicit scope, findings and evidence. Certification is blocked while any finding remains open.

This contract does not claim that an independent assessor has actually performed the work. The assessor identity and evidence references must come from the external assurance engagement.

The final assurance scope should include identity, authorization, policy, approvals, execution, sandboxing, secrets, interoperability, durability, observability, adversarial security, recovery, performance, supply chain, release controls and operational readiness.

After M29, the finite roadmap ends and the Platform enters continuous enterprise assurance.
