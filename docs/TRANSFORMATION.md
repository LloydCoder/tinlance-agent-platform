# P1 Transformation Contract

The Platform treats a Transformation as attributable business/execution context.

The record may describe requested capabilities, policy references, execution state, outputs, evidence and outcome, but those fields never authorize execution. The Platform's identity, authorization, policy, approval, budget, sandbox, secret and final side-effect controls remain authoritative.

A successful execution and a successful business outcome are separate states. This prevents technical completion from being reported as business success without outcome evidence.
